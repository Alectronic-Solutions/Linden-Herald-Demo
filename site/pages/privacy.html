This demonstration was built by Alectronic Solutions to show the proposed website and its features. It does not accept payments or process subscriptions, advertising orders or legal notices online.

Trying the contact form

The contact form is a demonstration only. Submitting it displays a notice in your browser; it does not send your message to the Herald or to Alectronic Solutions. Please use sample information and do not enter personal, financial or sensitive details.

Visiting this website

This demo does not include visitor analytics or advertising trackers. Its code does not set cookies or save form entries to browser storage. Your browser may retain information through its own autofill or history settings.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and requested pages, to deliver and protect the website. Those hosting practices are separate from the demo form.

Links and newspaper issues

Past newspaper issues open as PDFs. Links to other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.
