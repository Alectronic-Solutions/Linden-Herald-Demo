Legal notices in San Joaquin County

Need to publish a fictitious business name (DBA), name change, divorce or court summons notice? We publish legal notices for all of San Joaquin County, including Stockton, Lodi, Tracy, Manteca, Lathrop, Ripon, Escalon and Linden.

The San Joaquin County Superior Court in Sept. 1960 issued a decree (no. 72641) for The Linden Herald as a newspaper of general circulation, so notices published here meet the legal requirement.

How to publish your legal notice

  1. File your fictitious business name statement with the San Joaquin County Clerk, or get your court order.
  2. Call us at (209) 772-8854, any time of day, with your filed statement or court order nearby.
  3. We publish your notice on the dates the law or your court order requires, usually once a week for four weeks in a row.
  4. We file the Proof of Publication for you at no extra cost and send you a copy.
