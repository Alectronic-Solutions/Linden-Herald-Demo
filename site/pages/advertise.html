Legal notices in San Joaquin County

The San Joaquin County Superior Court in Sept. 1960 issued a decree (no. 72641) for The Linden Herald as a newspaper of general circulation. We can publish most legal notices including Fictitious Business Names, Change of Name Petition, Family Law, Trustee Sales, and Court Summons. Our prices are very competitive.
